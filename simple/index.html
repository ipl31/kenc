About

I build technology, bring people together, and follow my curiosity. That’s taken me from commercial fishing to community wireless networks, cloud infrastructure, and software for rocket launches.

My work has included engineering at Nebula, Oracle, Astra, and Turngate. Earlier, I helped build community wireless networks, connecting people with off-the-shelf radio gear and a lot of experimentation.

Outside of work I’m a dad, an amateur radio operator (KG7OFX), a cyclist and a disc golfer. Online you might know me as ModPackDad.
