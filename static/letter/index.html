The short version

I build technology, bring people together, and follow my curiosity.

That’s taken me from commercial fishing to community wireless networks, cloud infrastructure, and software for rocket launches.

These days, there’s also economics, games, fatherhood, and the occasional disc golf round. This is where the different threads meet.

A little more about me+

I helped build community wireless networks, connecting people using off-the-shelf radio gear and a lot of experimentation.

My work has included engineering at Nebula, Oracle, Astra, and Turngate. The scale changed; the pleasure of making something work remained.

Outside of work, I’m a dad, an amateur radio operator (KG7OFX), a cyclist, and a disc golfer. I’ve been interested in investing since 2008, and I’m still finding new questions in economics. You might also know me online as ModPackDad.
